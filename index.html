Our approach

From injury to back in play

Every patient moves through the same four phases. You don't move to the next phase until you pass its tests, not because a certain number of weeks have gone by.

  1. Evaluate

    A 60-minute one-on-one exam: injury history, movement, strength and range-of-motion testing.

    Visit 1

  2. Calm it down

    Reduce pain and swelling and restore motion with manual therapy and early exercise.

    Weeks 1–3

  3. Rebuild

    Progressive strength, balance and control work, tracked against your uninjured side.

    Weeks 3–8

  4. Return to play

    Sport-specific drills and testing (hop tests, sprint and cutting) before you're cleared.

    When you pass
