Services

What we treat

From a first-week sprain to a return to play after surgery, every plan starts with a full evaluation by a board-certified sports physical therapist.

ACL · Meniscus · Rotator cuff

Post-surgical rehab

Rehab that follows your surgeon's protocol, from early motion and swelling control to strength and return-to-sport testing.

Sprains · Strains · Tendinopathy

Sports injury care

Ankle sprains, hamstring strains, runner's knee, tennis elbow and other overuse injuries, treated early so you lose less time.

Gait · Cadence · Load

Running analysis

Video gait analysis and a training-load review to find what keeps causing shin splints, IT band pain or stress reactions.

Back · Neck · Hip · Shoulder

Training-related pain

Manual therapy and targeted exercise for back, neck, hip and shoulder pain that keeps you from training or competing.

SCAT6 · Vestibular

Concussion recovery

Guided, step-by-step return to activity with balance and vestibular testing and clear guidance on when you can play again.

Screening · Strength

Injury prevention

One-on-one movement screens and strength programs that address weak spots before they turn into injuries.

Our approach

From injury to back in play

Every patient moves through the same four phases. You don't move to the next phase until you pass its tests, not because a certain number of weeks have gone by.

  1. Evaluate

    A 60-minute one-on-one exam: injury history, movement, strength and range-of-motion testing.

    Visit 1

  2. Calm it down

    Reduce pain and swelling and restore motion with manual therapy and early exercise.

    Weeks 1–3

  3. Rebuild

    Progressive strength, balance and control work, tracked against your uninjured side.

    Weeks 3–8

  4. Return to play

    Sport-specific drills and testing (hop tests, sprint and cutting) before you're cleared.

    When you pass
